Cookie policy

This website does not use its own cookies for analytics, advertising, tracking or personalization purposes.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that may be stored on a user's device when they visit a website. They can be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, measure audience or show personalized advertising, among other uses.

Cookies used directly by this website

This website does not directly install analytics, advertising or tracking cookies in the user's browser.

Local storage (localStorage)

This website uses the browser's local storage (localStorage), not cookies, to remember technical preferences needed for it to work:

  • prob-theme: remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode.
  • prob-lang: remembers the language you chose (Spanish or English) so it can be shown to you automatically on future visits.

This data is stored only on your device, is never sent to any server or shared with third parties, and does not require consent since it is technical storage necessary for the site to work.

Technical server logs

The web hosting provider may generate technical logs necessary for the operation, security and maintenance of the site. These logs are not cookies installed by this website in the user's browser.

Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

This website may use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to obtain aggregated data about its presence in search engines, crawl errors, impressions, clicks and indexing performance.

These tools do not, by themselves, involve installing analytics cookies in the visitor's browser on behalf of this website.

Consent

Since this website does not directly install analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, a cookie consent banner is not currently shown.

If services that install non-technical cookies are added in the future, such as Google Analytics, AdSense, advertising pixels, maps, embedded videos, or advertising using tracking technologies, this policy will be updated and, where necessary, the corresponding consent will be requested.
