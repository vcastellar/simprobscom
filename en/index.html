Frequently asked questions

How are the results calculated?

All calculations run in your browser. The formulas, numerical approximations, assumptions of each method and the references used (NIST, SciPy, R) are documented on the methodology page. If a result is going to support an important decision, it is worth checking it against a second statistical package.
