Frequently asked questions

How are the results calculated?

All calculations run in your browser. Each calculator explains its formula, the approximation it uses and its assumptions; the methodology page covers the general criteria (rounding, numerical precision, simulation error) and the references used (NIST, SciPy, R). If a result is going to support an important decision, it is worth checking it against a second statistical package.

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

Yes: every tool is free and needs no sign-up or installation. Calculations run in your browser, so the data you enter is never sent to a server.

How do I find the right tool?

Use the search (Ctrl K) if you know the name of the distribution or test. If you are not sure, the “Which tool?” assistant gets you there in two or three questions, and each family has a guide with criteria for choosing and worked examples.
